# Global Travel Inspiration Platform v1

ONE WORLD ROUTE helps people discover remarkable journeys worldwide, understand routes, compare and save them, then plan and verify facts. It is not a booking portal, popularity feed or website for one scheduled expedition.

The 195-country One World Journey preserves 194 international transitions and its historic operational records. It is a showcase, with `defaultPlanningScenario` identifying the original example. A browser-local start date is planning context; route identity and source evidence remain immutable. Public timing is relative until a user chooses a date. Changing dates does not certify fares, timetables, entry conditions or seasonality. Readiness/HOLD/BLOCKED remains factual planning data for that journey, not a platform-release decision. Legacy internal audit names remain for compatibility.

## Discovery and visual behaviour

Inspire me samples only published catalog entries, excludes the previous suggestion when possible and makes no ML claim. Journey of the moment rotates deterministically by UTC day across published journeys. Recent trips use the existing local workspace, capped to its existing bounded history. No account, tracking service or invented popularity signals.

Globe paths have one default stroke; hover/selected/background state controls thickness and opacity. Region identity comes from catalog regions or optional visual metadata. Multi-continent legacy-preview arcs use the real endpoint country regions with a colour transition. Selection is accompanied by title, attributes and an Open Journey action. It reuses the existing globe instance and geometry.

Social Story is a browser-based 9:16-oriented presentation: discover, route, key stops, understand, explore. Facts come from the catalog/dataset; unknown budgets/distances are omitted. Copy caption/native share require user action. There is no auto posting, TikTok SDK or MP4 backend. SVG route illustration is explicitly not to scale. Images, when approved, remain illustrations rather than documentary evidence.

## Starting point policy

Existing `routePolicy`, eligible entry points, route starts and Traveller `originCountry` continue to drive regional entry suggestions. Distances are approximations and not booked access connections. Loops may rotate only through supported eligible stops. Open routes preserve logical endpoints or use explicit evidence-aware reversal. Cruises preserve the sourced/illustrative sailing sequence; origin access is separate. The world journey currently preserves its core order and allows dates, not unrestricted rotation. A future rotation adapter must keep segment IDs/evidence aligned, derive relative day offsets and recheck transport, entry, season and access assumptions. No automatic optimization is claimed.

## Future privacy-first analytics

Candidate events: journey_view, journey_open, journey_save, journey_compare, journey_share, social_story_open, finder_search, filter_use, planning_open. Prefer aggregate counts with consent where required; no passport/residence/origin details, precise location, persistent cross-site identifiers or full Traveller profiles. Do not call journeys trending/popular until real analytics supports a clearly described period and sample.

## Content and SEO

Keep own structured summaries, source links, checked dates, uncertainty and current-check-required states. Do not mirror foreign guides, blogs, articles or photography. Existing canonical, hreflang, TouristTrip JSON-LD and sitemap remain. Social is an acquisition channel; direct links and search pages stay independent of social platforms.

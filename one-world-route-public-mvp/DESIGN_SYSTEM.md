# Design system

## Direction

Mission control × premium travel visualization × restrained spatial glass UI.

## Principles

- The globe is the primary interface, not decoration.
- Glass is used for control surfaces, not every visual element.
- Red is reserved for real blockers/critical states.
- Route phase colors organize geography; operational layers can override them.
- Data confidence and uncertainty are visible rather than hidden.

## Core tokens

- Background: `#05070d`
- Glass: translucent navy with 24px blur
- Cyan: `#59ddff`
- Blue: `#4f7cff`
- Violet: `#9276ff`
- Amber: `#ffbf5a`
- Orange: `#ff7a45`
- Red: `#ff4d67`
- Green: `#65e5a7`

## Responsive behavior

Desktop: filters left, globe center, contextual details right, timeline bottom.
Mobile: globe remains central; left/right panels become drawers; phase rail stays scrollable.

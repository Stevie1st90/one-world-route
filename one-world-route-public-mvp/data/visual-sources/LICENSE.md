# Natural Earth relief source

Dataset: Natural Earth II 1:50m shaded relief and water, v3.2.0.

Original: https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/50m_raster/NE2_50M_SR_W.zip

Terms verified 2026-10-01: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Natural Earth declares its raster and vector data public domain and permits personal, educational and commercial use, modification and dissemination. No permission or attribution is required. We retain voluntary credit and provenance. The supplied WebP preserves the original 10800 × 5400 geographic grid; it was compressed from the source TIFF.

This is generalized relief and idealized land cover, not a detailed DEM or current satellite image. No other provider tiles were used to generate these assets.
